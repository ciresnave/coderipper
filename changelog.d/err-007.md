kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**ERR-007 is built: a resource is released on the error path too (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn`. In one block it pairs a `let x = ..` with a later statement that releases `x` by hand (`x.close()`, `.unlock()`, `.release()`, `.free()`, `.disconnect()`, or a call of `close`, `fclose`, `free`, `dealloc`, `CloseHandle`, `unlock` or `release` with `x`, `&x` or `&mut x` as an argument, optionally unwrapped, `?`-ed or awaited) and reports the release when a statement between the two holds a `?` or a `return` (outside a nested closure), because that early exit skips it. One finding per release at Medium severity and Low confidence, at the release call, subject `<file>:<function>:<resource>`. Low confidence because most Rust types release on drop, so the manual call is then redundant and not missing; the finding is the prompt to tie the release to scope exit. It is opt-in (`coderipper check ERR-007`): it does not see a guard or a `defer`-style crate, a release in another block or function, or a `break` that skips it. Fixtures `conformance/ERR-007/{defective,clean}`. Not measured on a corpus, so its precision is not claimed.
