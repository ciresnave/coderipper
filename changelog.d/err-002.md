kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**ERR-002 is built: a catch-all handler sits only at a boundary (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports a call of `catch_unwind` (`std::panic::catch_unwind`, `panic::catch_unwind`, a bare `catch_unwind`, or a `.catch_unwind()` method) in a function that is not a boundary: not `main`, not a function of a binary target (`src/main.rs`, `src/bin/..`), not a function with a boundary word among its `_`-separated words (`run`, `serve`, `handle`, `handler`, `dispatch`, `worker`, `listener`, `supervise`, `supervisor`, `boundary`, `entry`, `entrypoint`, `extern`, `ffi`, `callback`, `trampoline`: `run_server` is one, `truncate` is not), and not under a `//` comment on the call's line or in the three lines above that says why (`boundary`, `outermost`, `top-level`, `ffi`, `isolate`, `supervis`, `must not unwind`, `across the`). One finding per call at Medium severity and confidence, subject `<file>:<function>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check ERR-002`): the panic catcher is the only catch-all Rust has, so a swallowing `Err(_)` arm is `ERR-001`'s and is not read here, and a boundary the project declares elsewhere is not seen (allowlist it with the reason). Fixtures `conformance/ERR-002/{defective,clean}`. Not measured on a corpus, so its precision is not claimed.
