kind: Added: PRF-005 (opt-in, Rust)
---
**PRF-005 is built in part, Rust only, opt-in.** Run it by naming it, `coderipper check PRF-005`; it is in no profile. It reads production source with `syn` and reports **one Medium finding at low confidence per growable collection** (`Vec`, `VecDeque`, `HashMap`, `HashSet`, `BTreeMap`, `BTreeSet`, `BinaryHeap`, `LinkedList`, `IndexMap`, `IndexSet`, `DashMap`, `DashSet`, looked through `Mutex`, `RwLock`, `RefCell`, `Arc`, `Rc`, `LazyLock`, `OnceLock` and similar wrappers) that is a `static` or `thread_local!` static, or a field whose name contains `cache` or `memo` (not `memory`) (or any field of a struct named so), that is inserted into or pushed to and that nothing in the repository removes from, evicts, clears, truncates, reassigns, `mem::take`s or compares by `len()`. The finding is at the declaration; the allowlist subject is the static's name or `Owner::field`. Names are matched across the repository by identifier, so a bound on another type's field of the same name hides a candidate. Not read: whether the collection takes external input, growth or a bound through a returned reference, a macro or an alias the rule does not follow, types that bound themselves (`lru::LruCache`), locals and fields not named like a cache, tests; TypeScript and Python are not read. A cue to read, never a verdict. Fixtures `conformance/PRF-005/{clean,defective}`; the corpus counts are in the pull request. Patent sweep addendum: one idea, no hit read as claimed.
