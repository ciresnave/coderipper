kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**REL-002 is built: a retry loop has a cap and a delay that grows (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn`. A retry loop is a `loop`, `while` or `for` whose own body (a nested loop, closure or function is another unit) calls a function whose path ends in `sleep` and looks at a failure (an `Err(..)` match arm, an `if let Err(..)`, an `.is_err()` call). It reports one finding per loop at Medium severity and Low confidence, for either or both of: **no cap**, a `loop`, a `while` or a `for` over an endless iterator (`0..`, `repeat`, `cycle`) in which no comparison names an attempt counter or limit (an identifier holding `attempt`, `retr`, `tries`, `try`, `count`, `max`, `limit`, `budget`, `deadline`, `elapsed` or `timeout`); and **no backoff**, every sleep waiting a time written out in the call (literals and `SCREAMING_CASE` names only). Subject `<file>:<function>:<loop>@<line>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check REL-002`): it does not know whether the operation is safe to repeat, does not read jitter, a shared retry budget, retries stacked across layers or a retry crate, and a fixed delay held in a variable reads as growing. Fixtures `conformance/REL-002/{defective,clean}`. Not measured on a corpus, so its precision is not claimed.
