kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**EXT-001 is built: no catch-all arm over another file's enum (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and collects the package's own enums (an enum name held by two enums of the package, and an enum marked `#[non_exhaustive]`, are left out). It reports a `match` whose arms name variants of exactly one of them by a path with the enum's name (`Shape::Circle`, `crate::shape::Shape::Circle { .. }`, alone or in an or-pattern) when the `match` has an unguarded catch-all arm (`_`, or a lower-case binding) that absorbs at least one variant no other unguarded arm lists, and the `match` is in another file than the one that defines the enum (the file stands for the module). One finding per `match` at Medium severity and confidence, at the catch-all arm, naming the absorbed variants, subject `<file>:<function>:<enum>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check EXT-001`): it cannot tell a catch-all that is a deliberate default from one that is an oversight, and it does not see a bare variant name after `use Shape::*`, a `Self::` pattern, an enum of another crate, a tuple scrutinee, `if let` or `matches!`. A deliberate default is an allowlist entry with a reason, or a `#[non_exhaustive]` enum. Clippy's `wildcard_enum_match_arm` (a restriction lint, off by default) is the same idea at type-checker precision. Fixtures `conformance/EXT-001/{defective,clean}`; with `_` no longer read as a catch-all the conformance run fails (`the seeded defect was not reported at src/area.rs:6`). Measured on committed trees of eight of our repositories (2026-11-09): 102 sites in 6 repositories (Unpopped 54, auth-framework 36, synapse 8, PersistAnt 2, httpant 1, releaseant 1; the other two read clean), and what the sample shows is mostly matches that handle a few variants and default the rest on purpose, so the rule's precision as a defect finder is low and is not claimed; it is a list of every place that would absorb a new variant silently.
