kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**OPS-007 is built: every flag of a flag registry has an owner and a sunset date (any language, opt-in).** The rule reads tracked files named `flags`, `feature-flags`, `feature_flags`, `featureflags`, `feature-toggles`, `feature_toggles` or `toggles` as `.yml`, `.yaml`, `.json` or `.toml` (outside vendored, test, fixture and example directories). Its entries are the items of the list or map under a top-level `flags`, `feature_flags`, `features` or `toggles` key, or else of the file's own top-level list or map; a map entry that is not itself a map (`new_ui: true`) is a flag with nothing recorded. A flag is reported when it records no non-empty owner (`owner`, `owners`, `maintainer`, `team`, `responsible`, `contact`, `assignee`) or no non-empty sunset (`sunset`, `expires`, `expiry`, `expiration`, `expire`, `remove_by`, `removal_date`, `end_date`, `retire_by`, `delete_by`, `remove_after`). One finding per flag at Low severity and Medium confidence, at the line that names it, subject `<file>:<flag>`. A registry that does not parse is named in a note; a repository with no registry file is not applicable. It is opt-in (`coderipper check OPS-007`): it does not see flags declared in code or in a flag service, a registry under another name, whether a date is already past (that needs a clock) or whether a finished flag was deleted. Fixtures `conformance/OPS-007/{defective,clean}`. Not measured on a corpus, so its precision is not claimed.
