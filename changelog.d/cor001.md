---
**COR-001 is built, opt-in** (`coderipper check COR-001`; the public-surface reader of the 0.43 line, held out of that change for a patent review and shipped on its own). It reports a Rust `pub fn` that returns `Result` and has no `# Errors` section in its docs, a `pub unsafe fn` with no `# Safety` section, or a `pub fn` whose body names `panic!`, `unreachable!`, `unimplemented!`, `todo!` or `assert!`/`assert_eq!`/`assert_ne!` and has no `# Panics` section (Low severity, Medium confidence; `debug_assert!`, `unwrap()` and `expect()` are not read). Same public-surface definition as API-004, API-009 and ERR-004. It has fixtures under `conformance/COR-001` and a `checked_today` record.
**Measured on the corpus** (eight committed trees at 2026-11-06: coderipper, auth-framework, Unpopped, PersistAnt, configurant, httpant, releaseant, synapse): COR-001 reported 1,811 sites (auth-framework 1,159, synapse 349, PersistAnt 150, coderipper 27, releaseant 0), far too many to put in front of everyone, so it is **opt-in**. Its record in `docs/PATENT-SWEEP.md` is this version's addendum: the rule reads on claim 1 of US20060059457A1 as written, with two dated prior-art sources that we found and counsel has not reviewed; opt-in means it can be switched off if a patent owner objects.
