kind: Breaking: a new opt-in rule
---
**SEC-012 is built (Rust, opt-in): no `Debug` on a type that holds a secret.** A struct is reported, once and at its declaration, at Medium severity and Medium confidence, when it has a secret field that `Debug` prints: it derives `Debug`, or a hand-written `impl Debug` for it reads the field and shows no sign of redaction. A field is a secret when its name says so (`password`, `token`, `api_key`, `private_key`, `client_secret` and similar; a bare `key` is not read) or its type is `Zeroizing`; a companion word (`token_type`, `password_hash`, `key_id`) or a `bool`, number or `Duration` type says it is not. A field of type `Secret`, `SecretString` and the like is masked and not reported. Run it with `check SEC-012`; it is not in any profile yet.
