kind: Feature: new opt-in rule
---
**OPS-003 is built: a library writes no raw output (Rust, opt-in).** The tracked `.rs` files of a library package (one with `src/lib.rs` or a `[lib]` table) are parsed with `syn`, outside tests, examples, tools, `build.rs`, vendored trees, a binary's source (`main.rs`, `src/bin/`) and `#[cfg(test)]` code; each call of `println!`, `print!`, `eprintln!`, `eprint!` or `dbg!` (also path-qualified) is one finding at Low severity and High confidence, subject `<file>:<function>:<macro>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check OPS-003`): a print by a macro the package or a dependency defines, a hand-written write to `stdout()` or `stderr()`, a print inside another macro's arguments and other languages are not seen, and a library that prints by design (a command-line tool's support code) is a waiver with a reason. Fixtures `conformance/OPS-003/{defective,clean}`; with `println` removed from the macro list the conformance run fails, restored it reads proven. Measured on the committed trees (`HEAD`) of the local checkouts of eight of our repositories (2026-11-10, `check OPS-003 --project`; a checkout can lag `origin/main`): 341 sites in 6 repositories (coderipper 63, auth-framework 193, releaseant 52, synapse 26, PersistAnt 5, httpant 2; Unpopped and configurant 0). Most are the printing modules of a package that is a library and also ships a command (`src/cli.rs` holds 61 of coderipper's 63 and 43 of releaseant's 52; `src/admin/cli.rs` and `src/cli/mod.rs` hold 175 of auth-framework's 193) or conformance harness crates, which is why the rule stays opt-in. How many are defects to fix was not decided here, so the rule's precision is not claimed.
