kind: Feature: two new opt-in rules
---
**EFX-002 and CON-011 are built: process-wide mutable state (Rust, opt-in).** One scan reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports a `static mut`, and a `static` whose type names `Mutex`, `RwLock`, `RefCell`, `Cell`, `UnsafeCell`, `ArcSwap` or an `Atomic*` type (read through `OnceLock`, `LazyLock`, `Lazy`, `Arc` and `Box` to what they hold, so a lazily built `Regex` is not a site), including entries of `lazy_static!` and `thread_local!` and statics declared inside a function. **EFX-002** (only an allowlisted module owns global mutable state) reports each site at Medium severity and confidence; **CON-011** (shared state has a declared owner) reads the same sites and reports them at Low, so the two cannot disagree. Subject `<file>:<name>`. Both are opt-in (`coderipper check EFX-002`, `coderipper check CON-011`): no allowlist or owner inventory is read, so every site is a finding to remove or to waive with its owner named; a type alias that hides the lock, statics in other macros and other languages are not seen. Fixtures `conformance/{EFX-002,CON-011}/{defective,clean}`; with the `Mutex` and `RwLock` read blinded both conformance runs fail, and restored both read proven. Measured with `coderipper check EFX-002 --project` on the working trees of eight of our repositories (2026-11-10; heads coderipper 893e8f5, auth-framework 881c696d, Unpopped 8ad1f43, PersistAnt 7a6868a, configurant 6f6625f, httpant 466d8ad, releaseant 9a4be32, synapse 23e21e06; several trees had uncommitted changes, which this rule reads): 34 sites in 7 repositories (auth-framework 12, coderipper 11, PersistAnt 5, synapse 3, and one each in Unpopped, httpant and releaseant; configurant 0). By type: 20 atomics, 4 `Mutex`, 4 `Cell`, 3 `RefCell`, 3 `RwLock`. Most are counters and statistics (auth-framework's metrics collectors, coderipper's result-cache counters), a few are caches or registries, and several are ids and sequence numbers in test helpers that the file-level test exclusion does not cover (`testdb.rs`, a conformance harness). How many are defects to fix was not decided here, so the rules' precision is not claimed.
