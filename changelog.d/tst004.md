kind: Breaking: a new opt-in rule (TST-004)
---
**TST-004 is built, opt-in** (`coderipper check TST-004`; `src/module/neutral/tst004.rs`, the neutral module's Rust reading for `mockall`-style mocks). It reports a `#[test]` function that sets up at least **four** `expect_*` mock expectations and at least four times as many as it has assertions (a test that is mostly mock set-up), or that makes at least **three** `.times(..)` call-count checks and has no assertion (a test that only verifies who called whom). Low severity, Low confidence; `Result::expect_err` is not counted. The thresholds are the rule's own and not configurable yet. It is **contested** in the catalog (mockist teams verify interactions on purpose) and it cannot tell an internal collaborator from a boundary the project does not own, so it runs only when named. Mocks from other libraries, hand-written fakes and a mock built in a helper are not seen (`checked_today`).
**Proven and measured.** `coderipper conformance --module neutral --rule TST-004` is proven by the fixtures in `conformance/TST-004`, and two mutations of the rule turn it red (the mock-heavy branch removed: the seeded defect is not reported; the assertion ratio removed: the clean twin is reported). **Corpus** (eight committed trees at 2026-11-07, 1,320 tracked Rust files outside fixture directories: coderipper, auth-framework, Unpopped, PersistAnt, configurant, httpant, releaseant, synapse): **0 sites**, and **none of the eight depends on `mockall`**, so the zero shows the rule does not misfire on code with no mocks (it did count `Result::expect_err` as a set-up until a search of the corpus for `expect_` calls turned up `expect_err`) and says nothing about how it reads real `mockall` tests; that is why it is opt-in.
