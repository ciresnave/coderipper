kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**TYP-005 is built: money is not held in a binary float (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports a named struct or enum-variant field, a function parameter, a `let` binding with a type annotation, or a function return whose name has a word `price`, `cost`, `balance`, `fee`, `salary`, `wage`, `payment`, `amount`, `money`, `revenue` or `charge` (a plural counts) and whose type, as written, is `f32` or `f64` (also inside `Option`, `Vec`, a reference or a tuple). A name with a word such as `rate`, `ratio`, `percent`, `factor`, `scale`, `weight` or `score` is left out. One finding per site at Medium severity and Low confidence. It is opt-in (`coderipper check TYP-005`): it guesses from names, not from module tags, so a quantity named like money that is not money is reported too. Fixtures `conformance/TYP-005/{defective,clean}`. Measured on committed trees of eight of our repositories (2026-11-06): 3 sites in 2 repositories; one is a money amount (`transaction_amount: f64` in a payment-authentication request), two are a routing `cost` metric of a transport (not money). Nothing was tuned on that reading, so the rule stays opt-in and its precision is about one site in three on this corpus.
