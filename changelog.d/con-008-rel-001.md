kind: Feature: two new opt-in rules
---
**CON-008 and REL-001 are built: outbound calls and awaits carry a deadline (Rust, opt-in).** One scan reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and finds three kinds of site: an HTTP client built without a timeout (`reqwest::Client::new()`, `reqwest::get(..)` and the `blocking` forms, or a `Client::builder()` / `ClientBuilder::new()` chain ending in `.build()` with no `.timeout(..)`, `.connect_timeout(..)` or `.read_timeout(..)` in it; a bare `Client` counts when the file imports it from `reqwest`), an awaited `TcpStream::connect(..).await` (tokio, async-std, smol) that is not inside a call of `timeout(..)` or `timeout_at(..)`, and a blocking `std::net::TcpStream::connect(..)` (`connect_timeout` is the call that has a deadline). **REL-001** (outbound calls have deadlines) reports all three kinds; **CON-008** (an await on something outside the process has a timeout) reports the awaited connects only, so the two rules read the same sites and cannot disagree. One finding per site at Medium severity and confidence, subject `<file>:<function>:<what>`. Both are opt-in (`coderipper check REL-001`, `coderipper check CON-008`): a request sent with `.send().await` on a client built elsewhere, a deadline applied by a wrapper or a middleware layer, database and RPC clients and a timeout set from the environment are not seen, and a call that waits on purpose is a waiver with a reason (the rules say exceptions are declared). Fixtures `conformance/{CON-008,REL-001}/{defective,clean}`; with the awaited-connect read switched off only the CON-008 conformance run fails, and with `Client::new()` unread only the REL-001 run fails. Measured on committed trees of eight of our repositories (2026-11-10): REL-001 reports 31 sites in 3 repositories (19 in one, 8 in another, 4 in the third), CON-008 reports 9 sites in 2 repositories (8 and 1). The sites in the repository with 19 and in the one with 4 are mostly `reqwest::Client::new()` in code that calls external services in production; the 8 in the other are connects of its own bench and conformance harnesses to a local server, which a deadline would also bound but where a stall is a test failure and not an outage. How many are defects to fix was not decided here, so the rules' precision is not claimed.
