kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**CON-005 is built: no detached spawned task without a written reason (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports a call of a free spawn function (`tokio::spawn`, `task::spawn`, `task::spawn_local`, `spawn_blocking`, `thread::spawn`, `std::thread::spawn`, an `async_std` or `smol` spawn, or a bare `spawn(..)` that the file imports from one of them, a rename included) whose handle is dropped on the spot: the call is a statement of its own, or is bound to `_`. A handle kept in a variable (`let handle = ..`, `let _task = ..`), returned, pushed, awaited or joined is not reported, nor is a spawn through a method (a `JoinSet`, a scope), where a set or a scope owns the task. A detached spawn with a justification is not reported either: a `//` comment on the call's line, or in the comment block directly above it, that says why (`detach`, `fire-and-forget`, `background`, `on purpose`, `deliberate`, `intentional`, `supervis`, `owned by`). Functions under `#[cfg(test)]` or any `#[..::test]` attribute are skipped. One finding per detached spawn at Medium severity and confidence, subject `<file>:<function>:<callee>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check CON-005`): it sees the dropped handle, not whether the task forwards its own errors, and a handle that is kept and never joined, or a spawn through a wrapper of the project, is not seen. Fixtures `conformance/CON-005/{defective,clean}`; with expression-statement spawns no longer read, the conformance run fails (`the seeded defect was not reported at src/lib.rs:4`). Measured on committed trees of eight of our repositories (2026-11-09): 72 sites in 6 repositories (synapse 41, httpant 14, configurant 6, auth-framework 6, coderipper 4, PersistAnt 1; Unpopped and releaseant read clean). Three sampled sites are deliberate (a watchdog thread, a stdio pump thread, a periodic scan loop) without a reason written beside them in the words the rule reads, which is what it asks to be written; how many of the 72 are defects was not measured, so the rule's precision is not claimed.
