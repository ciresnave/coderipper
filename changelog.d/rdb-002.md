kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**RDB-002 is built: names follow the Rust naming conventions (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports a function, method, `let` binding or parameter name with an upper-case letter, a struct, enum, union, trait, type alias or enum variant name that starts in lower case or has a `__` or an underscore beside a letter, and a `const` or `static` name with a lower-case letter. A leading `_` and `r#` are ignored. Items under `#[allow(non_snake_case)]`, `non_camel_case_types`, `non_upper_case_globals` or `nonstandard_style` (also as an inner attribute), `#[no_mangle]` and `#[export_name]` items, `extern` blocks, trait declarations and trait-impl methods, and code inside macro invocations are not read. One finding per name at Low severity and High confidence, at the name, subject `<file>:<kind>:<name>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check RDB-002`): rustc's `non_snake_case`, `non_camel_case_types` and `non_upper_case_globals` lints are the same idea at type-checker precision and warn by default, so the rule's use is a repository that has silenced them or a tree read without a build; it does not read a project's own conventions or an acronym style. Fixtures `conformance/RDB-002/{defective,clean}`.
