kind: Feature: a new opt-in rule
---
**ERR-011 is built: no parse whose failure is silently replaced by a default (Rust, opt-in).** The rule reads the tracked `.rs` files outside tests with `syn` and reports `text.parse()` (any turbofish, no arguments) or `T::from_str(text)`, with or without `.ok()` after it, whose failure is replaced on the spot by `.unwrap_or_default()`, `.unwrap_or(..)` or `.unwrap_or_else(..)`: the failure signal is thrown away, so a malformed input flows onward as if it were real data. A comment on the line, or in the comment block directly above it, that says why (`default`, `fall back`, `fallback`, `lenient`, `best effort`, `on purpose`, `deliberate`, `intentional`) silences the site, and functions under `#[cfg(test)]` or any `#[..::test]` attribute are skipped. One finding per site at Medium severity and confidence, subject `<file>:<function>:<parse or from_str>`. It is opt-in (`coderipper check ERR-011`): `syn` has no types, so a zero-argument `.parse()` is read as `str::parse`, a parse that is `.ok()`-ed and tested later, one wrapped in a function of the project, and a default that is the right answer for an optional setting are not told apart. Fixtures `conformance/ERR-011/{defective,clean}`.
