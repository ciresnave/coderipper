**Idea {{IDEA1}} (`ARC-011`).** For each crate, take the pairs of its top-level modules whose source names each other (each refers to the other), read from the committed history the commits that touched a file of each, and report a pair that was changed together in at least a minimum number of commits and in a large share of the commits that touched either, at Info and low confidence: two modules that refer to each other and always change together are one module split in two.
