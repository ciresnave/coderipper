All assignee STMicroelectronics SRL, priority 2020-07-24, status per Google Patents (not the official record): US12524655B2 active, est. expiry 2044-07-11; EP3968235B1 active; CN113971456B active; IT202000018043A1 is the national priority application (no grant).
**No continuation or divisional was found**: the page's text has no "continuation" or "divisional" entry, and "Families Citing this family (8)" are citing documents (different applicants and priorities), not family members. Not checked: USPTO continuity data, EPO register (divisionals), a later US filing that claims this priority but is not listed by Google Patents.
Independent claims read in full, every one carries both limits the PM named:
- **US12524655B2 claims 1, 8, 16, 18**: claim 1 and 8 recite solving an optimization problem with a first term that makes normalized weights approximate the output activation as a function of the input activation, and a regularization term ΩL(u) amplifying weights by distance from the lattice points. Claims 16 and 18 (stored-values systems with a hardware accelerator or circuitry that decompress and run the ANN) recite the same two terms ("correspond to a solution to an optimization problem having ... a first term ... and a regularization term ... based on distances of weight values from lattice points"); claim 18 also classifies an image. Claim 18 and 16 drop the symbol ΩL and the vectorize/normalize/LVQ/index steps (those are in dependents 17 and 19) but NOT the two terms.
- **EP3968235B1 claim 1** (computer-implemented method): same steps plus the optimization with both terms (regularization "closer to, having a reduced distance from, said lattice points"). The other independent-form claims (computer program product, medium, accelerator-engine method 12, engine circuit) refer to claim 1's method ("any of claims 1 to 9") and so inherit its limits. Not read: any claim past those in the tool's text.
- **CN113971456B claims 1, 12, 16, 27, 29** (machine translation; "lattice" is rendered "grid"): each recites the optimization with the same first term and a regularization term amplifying values by distance from the grid points; claims 1, 12, 16, 27 and 29 each also require classifying an image with the ANN (image processing), narrower than the US claims.
- **IT202000018043A1** (national priority application, machine translation, as filed): independent claim 1 is the method with both terms (Italian "ottimizzazione" and "regolarizzazione"); 9, 12, 15 depend on claim 1's method.
**Answer to "does any family member drop the limits": none found.** Every independent claim read recites both the activation-approximation term and the lattice-distance regularization term. Read against ml-convertant's IQ3_S, IQ2_* and IQ3_XXS (fixed published grid, signs, block scale; encode or dequantize only; no optimization against activations, no lattice-distance regularizer, no vectorize-normalize-LVQ pipeline for an NPU): outside as read on both limits. **Verdict change for the PM:** from "needs-review" (my first record, claim 1 read in part) to "clear as read for the fixed-grid types, conditional": a converter that did the activation-approximating optimization with a distance-to-lattice regularizer for its own codebook would read on claim 1 and would need the review.
